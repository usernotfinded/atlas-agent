## What v0.6.27 left open

`v0.6.27` was a safety-correction release, and the honest reading of it is that
it closed the defects that were **found**, not the class they belong to. Seven of
its eight defects had one shape: a consumer reading one of two sources describing
the same safety state. That shape is a search, not a fix, and nothing in the
repository currently searches for it.

Five items were recorded as decisions rather than corrections, in
[safety-invariant-audit-followups](../development/safety-invariant-audit-followups.md).
Two of them are the most likely content of this line:

- **The heartbeat commands feed a `DeadmanSwitch` that nothing constructs.**
  286 lines, 12 methods, 11 tests, and no caller. Wiring it is not a code change
  so much as a decision about who may assert liveness, and it interacts with the
  recorded fact that an absent heartbeat reads as fresh — so a path that evaluates
  without recording inherits a deadman that cannot fire.
- **The emergency flatten cannot be reached by escalating.** Deliberately
  reserved for external broker-adapter review rather than decided here.

## Safety boundaries

This planning line enables nothing at runtime. Specifically, no candidate or
document in `v0.6.28` planning:

- enables live trading, live submit, or `atlas run --mode live`;
- enables broker or provider execution, or adds a direct AI-to-broker path;
- places, cancels, or flattens orders;
- creates pending orders or mutates approval queues;
- loads credentials, secrets, or API keys;
- adds network access;
- bypasses `RiskManager`, approval queues, the kill switch, the heartbeat, the
  deadman, the audit hash-chain, live-submit opt-in, or `can_submit`;
- moves any default toward permissiveness.

L4 unattended live autonomy remains out of reach without external legal,
security, operational, risk, broker-adapter and regulatory review by qualified
parties, which
[Bounded Live Autonomy Governance](../bounded-live-autonomy-governance.md)
requires to be done by them and not by self-assessment.
